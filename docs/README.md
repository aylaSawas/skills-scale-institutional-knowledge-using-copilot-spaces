# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository centralizes the core program and project management practices OctoAcme uses to initiate, plan, execute, release, and improve work. OctoAcme follows a customer-first, iterative, data-informed approach with clear ownership and a consistent cadence for communication, delivery, and learning. Work moves through a structured lifecycle: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective/continuous improvement.

## Overview of OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme starts each effort by validating the business need and confirming measurable outcomes during project initiation. Teams document a project one-pager, identify stakeholders, define success criteria, and decide whether to move forward with planning. Once approved, planning turns the initiative into an actionable backlog, timeline, and milestone plan. This includes prioritizing work, estimating scope, defining the Definition of Done, and tracking dependencies and risks that could affect the delivery plan.

During execution, the team works in small, testable increments and uses regular touchpoints to keep work visible and unblock issues quickly. Daily standups and weekly delivery reviews help teams surface progress, blockers, and cross-team dependencies. The project board is used to track work through stages such as Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, and Done. OctoAcme also expects clear role ownership: the Project Manager coordinates delivery and communication, the Product Manager defines outcomes and prioritization, developers implement and validate features, QA confirms readiness, and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Quality and risk management are built into the workflow. Pull requests should be small, link to the issue and acceptance criteria, and pass CI checks before merge. Unit, integration, and smoke tests are expected based on the change, and security scans plus manual QA help ensure release readiness. Risks and dependencies are tracked in a risk register, reviewed in regular syncs, and escalated through defined paths when issues become business-impacting. Release management follows structured pre-deployment readiness steps, smoke tests, and post-deploy verification to reduce production risk.

OctoAcme closes the loop through retrospectives and continuous improvement. After each sprint, release, or milestone, the team captures what went well, what could improve, and concrete action items with owners and due dates. These improvements are folded back into the backlog and reviewed in ongoing communication rhythms so the team becomes more effective over time.
